The start of a new year invites reflection, not expectation.

It’s a moment to pause. Not to fix yourself or rush into change, but to take stock of what’s carried you here and what you want to hold onto as you move forward.

The past few years have been heavy for a lot of people. Things haven’t always been easy, and priorities have shifted in ways we didn’t expect. Through all of that, many of you have continued to walk through our doors, message us, talk with our team, and trust us with something personal. We don’t take that lightly.

If you’ve been with us for a while, you’ll likely recognise faces in our shops or have spoken with members of our team, and some of you know our owners too. Those conversations matter to us. We’ve seen people come in unsure and leave more confident. We’ve watched customers grow, change, and settle into themselves. Over time, many of those interactions have turned into genuine relationships, and in some cases, real friendships.

Scandals is an independent UK business. We’re rooted in our local community and proud to employ people from the area we call home. The people you speak to aren’t scripts or bots. They’re real, thoughtful, and genuinely care about doing things properly. Respect, honesty, and trust aren’t marketing ideas for us. They’re how we try to work every day.

Over the last year, we’ve been more careful about how we operate. We’ve chosen to focus on what we do well and let go of things that stretched us too thin. Protecting the wellbeing and energy of our team has been a priority, because when people feel supported, they show up better for each other and for you.

That approach has paid off. Despite a tough economic climate, this has been our strongest year in recent times. Not because we chased growth for the sake of it, but because we stayed steady, made thoughtful choices, and built on the trust we’ve earned together.

Looking ahead, our intention is simple. We’ll keep listening. We’ll keep improving. And we’ll continue bringing in new products and ideas that feel right, chosen with care, inclusivity, and real lives in mind.
