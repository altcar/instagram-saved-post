$30AUD wagyu steak with unlimited fries 🍟 This restaurant is run by the team behind Operator Diner, and they’ve gone all in on a steak-and-fries-only concept😎You first pick one of four sauces, then they give you a card to signal whether you want more fries or you’ve had enough — flip it to “more fries” and the refills keep coming. Don’t miss the tableside tiramisu either; the scoop is insanely generous😍
