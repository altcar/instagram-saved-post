The Viral Lazy Tiramisu 🥮

A very practical dessert ready in 5 minutes 🙌

Honestly, we were a little hesitant at first while making it, but after it was finished, the taste was so good…

I preferred Biscoff biscuits, but you can also make it with any other. 👌

Ingredients;

Cream cheese
Half a cup of coffee (sweetened)
Biscoff Biscuits
Cocoa for topping

*Reklam değil.
