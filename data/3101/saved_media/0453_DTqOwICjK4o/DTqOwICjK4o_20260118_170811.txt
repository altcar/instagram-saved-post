Latest research suggests people with breasts are less likely to receive CPR than men (68% Vs 73%). Data suggests a third a men would be concerned of being accused of inappropriate touching if they gave CPR.

That's why we've teamed up with @revivehercpr to provide more realistic training on delivering CPR to women - reminding everyone that most CPR mannequins only represent half the population. 

We've already started using these in our training sessions, and hope to continue teaching the importance of being to give CPR to anyone, regardless of body type. 

Want to get involved? We meet on Wednesdays at Mile End campus, open to all students studying at QM!
