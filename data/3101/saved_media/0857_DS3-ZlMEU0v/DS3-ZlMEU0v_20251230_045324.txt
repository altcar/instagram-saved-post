直說了。覺得失控，問題不是速度，是轉彎形狀。直直往落線滑，看起來又快又可怕。把轉彎拉到橫越坡面，才會有控制。速度來自落線，控制來自轉彎形狀。感謝我的學生 @albertx1229 和 @hhhborder 分享他們的滑行影片，讓大家一起學習 🙏

Hot take. If you feel out of control, it’s not speed, it’s turn shape. Skidding straight down the fall line feels fast and scary. Shaping turns across the hill gives control. Speed comes from the fall line. Control comes from turn shape. Thanks to my students @albertx1229 and @hhhborder for sharing their riding so we can all learn 🙏
