The 2003 Dior Fall-Winter ready-to-wear show in Paris, under then-creative director John Galliano, was famously crashed by an animal rights activist.

A female protester from PETA (People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals) abruptly jumped onto the catwalk, disrupting the presentation. She carried a sign that read “FUR SHAME” and chanted against the use of animal fur in fashion. Security quickly intervened and forcefully removed her from the runway.

The incident was notable not only for the disruption but also because the very next model to walk, following the protest, was wearing a dramatic lilac fur coat as part of Galliano’s opulent collection. This juxtaposition highlighted the clash between the anti-fur movement and the luxury fashion industry’s continued use of the material. Despite the ruckus, the show carried on immediately.
