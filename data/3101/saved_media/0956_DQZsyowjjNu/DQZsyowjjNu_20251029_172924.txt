Throwback to the Cara Delevingne Tumblr era
(2012-2015)

Starting as a British model, Cara Delevingne became one of the most recognizable faces of the early 2010s. Using Tumblr to build her image beyond magazines and runways, she connected with the online “swag era” by posting unfiltered photos, joking around in interviews, and showing personality when most models didn’t.

Her gifs, memes, and fan blogs spread fast, and her signature look of thick eyebrows, beanies, and streetwear fit perfectly into the internet culture at the time.
