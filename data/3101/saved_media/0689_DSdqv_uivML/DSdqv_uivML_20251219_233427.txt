Matcha earl grey tiramisu recipe
Whoever thought of this combo I love you 

Recipe: 

Earl grey mascarpone cream
- 3 egg yolks
- 80g granulated sugar
- 3 earl grey tea bags, cut open 
- 1tsp vanilla 
- Pinch salt
- 230g (8oz) mascarpone 
- 300g heavy cream, chilled 

- A pack of ladyfingers (you won’t use all)
Matcha soak
- 7g matcha powder sifted 
- 70g warm water
- 2tsp sugar 

Filling:
1. In a blender, blitz the granulated sugar and earl grey until it becomes a fine powder
2. Add the blitzed sugar + earl grey mixture to the egg yolks and place over a double boiler. (The bottom of the bowl shouldn’t touch the water). Whisk until doubled in size 
3. Add the vanilla and salt and set aside.
4. To another bowl, add the mascarpone and heavy cream. Whip until medium peaks form (do not over-whip otherwise it will become grainy)
5. Add the egg yolk mixture to the cream and gently fold until incorporated. 

Matcha soak:
1. Sift the matcha powder and whisk (preferably with a bamboo whisk or a hand frother) with the warm water and sugar.

Assembly:
1. Dip each ladyfinger into the matcha soak and cover the bottom layer of the baking dish. Optionally sift a layer of matcha powder before layering the cream 
2. Add 1/2 of the cream mixture and evenly spread over using a spatula. 
3. Repeat steps 1-2 once more 
4. Chill for at least 5 hours, ideally overnight 
5. Before serving, dust with matcha and enjoy
