**Rose Pasta Recipe**

**Ingredients**

* Pasta
* 7–10 tomatoes
* Shrimp
* 1 onion
* Spinach
* Minced garlic
* 4 tbsp tomato sauce
* Cream cheese
* 1.5 tbsp chicken stock
* 400 ml water
* 200 ml milk

**Instructions**

1. Add the pasta, tomatoes, shrimp, onion, spinach, minced garlic, tomato sauce, cream cheese, chicken stock, water, and milk to a pot. Bring to a boil and cook together.
 (If you don’t want overcooked shrimp, remove them once they are cooked.)

2. When the pasta is almost fully cooked, add butter and cheese, then simmer until the sauce thickens. Serve and enjoy.
