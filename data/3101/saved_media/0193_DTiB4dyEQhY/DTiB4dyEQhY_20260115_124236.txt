「時光大耳窿」

內容以主角三人從老年生活為開始，
再以各種以時間為概念的拍攝手法，
倒退回去中年與青年時期。
老年時期使用了長達1分鐘40秒的一鏡到底， 
中年時期轉換成青年時期，
使用了Stop Motion作為敘事方式，
像掉入了另一個時空把三人帶回青年時期。
這部MV從舞蹈、走位、拍攝手法都是前期互相配合再執行，
為這一首歌曲量身訂作，把這一首歌的故事配合畫面完整帶給觀眾。

The story begins with the three main characters in their old age.
Through various time-based visual techniques, the narrative then rewinds back to their middle-aged and youth periods.

The elderly stage is presented in a single one long take lasting 1 minute and 40 seconds.
As the story transitions from middle age to youth, stop-motion is used as a visual storytelling technique, as if the characters fall into another time dimension that brings the three of them back to their younger selves.

This music video is meticulously designed in advance, with choreography, blocking, and filming techniques developed in close coordination before execution.
It is tailor-made for this song, allowing the story of the music to be fully conveyed to the audience through visuals.
