MY 2025 WRAPPED⭐️❤️

2025 has been the BEST year of my life, but also one of the most challenging.

I have been having a hard time with my mental health recently, and feeling proud of myself has never come easily, but taking the time to journal and sit with myself—reflecting month by month—has helped me conceptualize the immense growth and progress I have made this past year. This post serves to reflect on the incredible wins I have had in 2025, where the personal ones count just as much as the professional ones. 

At the beginning of each year, I select quadrant(s) of my life to focus on. This year was about career and personal growth. All my friends and family know how goal-oriented I am; I have a vision board and Notes list that details every career goal I set out for myself in 2025, and I am in disbelief that I have achieved every. Single. One. It’s easy to get caught up chasing what’s next, but if 2025 has taught me anything, it’s to enjoy the moment, take time to reflect, and be proud of the miles I’ve walked to get here. 

On a personal level, this year, I finally recognized and processed trauma that I have carried for the past 20 years. It’s been the hardest thing I’ve ever had to do (running is a hell of a lot easier than remembering), but also what I know is necessary to become an even stronger and better person. This year also made me more aware of the incredible privilege I have. I feel lucky to have a platform like this one to keep sharing my journey, as I hope it inspires other young people. 

You are stronger than you think. You deserve to be here, on this Earth. You deserve to achieve your dreams, be loved and give love, and celebrate all your wins. 

Here’s to even more learning, happiness, and impact in 2026. 🥂
