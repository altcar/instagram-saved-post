Imagine coming in for a smooth landing after a long flight and the welcome party is a kangaroo with hands up ready to box. A paraglider touched down near Canberra and before he could even unclip his gear a kangaroo ran straight toward him and threw punches. He even tried greeting it at first before realizing this was not a friendly hello at all. The whole thing was caught on camera and it looked like a scene from a wild nature show happening in real life.

The paraglider was not seriously hurt and the kangaroo eventually hopped away but it shows how fast wildlife can flip the mood. One moment calm landing. Next moment you are squaring up with an animal that clearly lives there and does not care that you do not. You can hear the surprise in his voice as he realizes this is really happening right now.
