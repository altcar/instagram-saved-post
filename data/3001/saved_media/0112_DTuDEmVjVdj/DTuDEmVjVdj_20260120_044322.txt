Cryotherapy can be powerful for relieving stress and depression because extreme cold triggers the release of endorphins, the body’s natural mood boosters. The sudden temperature change also helps lower inflammation and cortisol levels, which are closely linked to chronic stress and low mood. Many people report feeling mentally refreshed and energized after a session, as the cold shock can reset the nervous system and improve sleep quality. Together, these effects create a fast, physical way to support emotional balance and mental clarity.
