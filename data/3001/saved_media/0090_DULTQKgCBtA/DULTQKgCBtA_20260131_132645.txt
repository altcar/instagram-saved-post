We ordered:
- Hot oil chicken leg belt noodles £14.8
- Hot oil crispy tofu belt noodles £13.8
- 5 beef skewers £8.8
- Garlic and pepper corn £6.8
- Grilled scallop £4.4
- Grilled enoki mushroom £8.8
- Beef tongue skewer £2.2 
- Lychee juice £2.4

The noodles were great and chewy, but I felt the sauce lacked the authentic Chinese hot oil flavour and spice to it, flavour is a bit white but still nice though, and portions were very generous especially the huge chicken! 😌😌
The skewers and corn smells v good and I would rec not wearing a sweater lmao, do think skewer meat can be a bit more fresh but very well seasoned and delish!! Would skip the enoki mushroom and scallops, again sauce can be improved. We left very full and the variety of food incl sides offered is also great!
