The Lighthouse • Winter 26

For Winter 26, we ventured across land and sea to the lighthouse - a magical and almost mythic place on the Scottish Isle of Skye.

Standing amid jagged rocks, framed by mountains on a headland, we found our inspiration in Ornsay Lighthouse. With its masonry tower and galley, lantern and keeper’s house, it was the lighthouse we had pictured in our dreams.

Coincidentally, Ornsay’s cottages were once owned by the author, Gavin Maxwell, whose novel ‘Ring of Bright Water’ has long been one of our favourites.

Come with us as we explore Skye’s mesmerising waters, its labyrinthine lochs, sea arches, craggy shores and towering cliffs.
