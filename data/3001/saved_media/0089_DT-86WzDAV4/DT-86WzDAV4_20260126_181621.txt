Belt Noodles🍜🍗🌶️
📍Rupert Street, London

Londons longest and widest belt noodles👀

This place is bloody lush!!! We took a trip to London over the weekend and were lucky enough to try out some delicious food spots!

@beltnoodles thoroughly impressed us! They were packed with flavour and absolutely MASSIVE!

We opted for a beef one and a chicken one and they were both just as lush as each other! Spicy and super hearty, you literally can’t ask for much more - we also got a couple of skewers and some spring rolls which delish😍

We cannot recommend this place enough, if you’re ever in London you NEED to try this place out🔥
