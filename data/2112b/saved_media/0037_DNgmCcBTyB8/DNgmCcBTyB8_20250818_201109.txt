Every day, the safety of our children is paramount. As part of our commitment to ensuring their well-being, we launch this campaign to raise awareness about protecting children from strangers. It is our collective responsibility to educate and empower our young ones with the knowledge and tools they need to stay safe.

Key Points:
1. Recognize Safe Spaces: Teach children about safe places, like their homes, schools, and trusted community centers.
2. Stranger Danger: Instill the importance of not talking to, accepting gifts from, or going anywhere with strangers.
3. Emergency Contacts: Ensure kids know their emergency contacts, such as parents, guardians, and emergency services.
4. Stay Together: Encourage children to stay in groups and never wander alone in public areas.
5. Open Communication: Foster an environment where children feel comfortable discussing their experiences and concerns.
Together, we can create a safer world for our children.
