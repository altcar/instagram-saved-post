Go viral using these 3 proven hooks ⬇️

1️⃣ Stitch Hook: Borrow momentum from a trending clip and add your unique take. It instantly taps into curiosity and context your audience already cares about.

2️⃣ Shock Value Hook: Say something that makes people pause: a bold stat, a surprising truth, or a statement that feels almost controversial (but still true). The goal is to disrupt their scroll.

3️⃣ Visual Hook: Let the first frame do the talking. A chase scene, a dramatic gesture, or even text that fills the screen can make people stop before they even hear a word.

The best Creators don’t just post content—they engineer attention. Master these 3 hooks and you’ll see the difference in your reach :)
