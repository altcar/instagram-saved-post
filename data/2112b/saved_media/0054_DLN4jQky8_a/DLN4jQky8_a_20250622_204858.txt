✋🔠 i made a scrappy and yet fun font editor ✨
pinch-rotate like a knob that switches modes:
👌 index+thumb → pick the letter
🤌 index+middle+thumb → warp its glyphs
🖐️ all five → smooth the lines

shoutout to @schultzschultzgrafik - the “pinch rotate switch modes” ui mechanism is inspired by their touchtype 👏

also another experiment of using @touchdesigner ‘s new op family pops to manipulate lines/curves ✨
