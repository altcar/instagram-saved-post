💅 “I’m a Barbie girl, in the Barbie world…”
Released in April 1997, Aqua’s “Barbie Girl” became a global sensation with its bubblegum pop sound, cheeky lyrics, and unforgettable music video.
The song topped charts in over 10 countries and became a defining anthem of late ’90s Europop.

📚 Fun fact: The song sparked a major lawsuit from Mattel, the makers of Barbie, who claimed it damaged the doll’s image. Aqua responded by calling the song a satirical social commentary—and the court agreed.
Decades later, Mattel embraced the track, licensing it for the 2023 Barbie movie soundtrack.
