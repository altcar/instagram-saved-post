⬇️The idea of a 18-year-old choosing their major *for the rest of their life* is… somewhat ridiculous, don’t you think?

If you feel like your major is draining you — you’re not alone.

Entering my undergrad, I decided to go to law school. At first, law felt so right:
❤️‍🔥 truly what interests me
📝 aligns with my strengths
🫂 helps people
💸 lucrative long-term

But in a year everything changed…
When job search started.

That’s when I felt the downsides:
🚫 I’m in undergrad and can’t work in the field yet
💰 Law school = *major* debt
⏳ 5 more years until a full-time paycheck
📚 LSAT prep + apps = expensive

And it’s a stretch.

This made me question everything.

And I think I’ll still pursue law — but I’m also searching for ways to pivot — just in case the downsides will start to weight on too much.

🎓 Anyone else rethinking their path lately? I’d love to hear your story too.

I started this blog to be honest about the parts of student life we don’t always talk about. No “looking back” wisdom — just the things I don’t hear people say out loud.
