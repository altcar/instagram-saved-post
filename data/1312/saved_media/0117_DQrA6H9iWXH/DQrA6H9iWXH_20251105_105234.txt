All you have to do is add water and boil! Why are you guys paying so much for instant hotpot with all those dehydrated and preserved vegetables?! Make this instead and keep it in your freezer for rainy days. 😍🍲

Miso Soup Base: 
1 tbsp dashi soup stock (or chicken stock)
2 tbsp Miso
1 tbsp Soy sauce
1 tbsp sake or Mirin 
1 tbsp brown sugar
