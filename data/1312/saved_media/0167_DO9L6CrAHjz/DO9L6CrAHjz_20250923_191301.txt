Day 23 — turning Kode Dot into an air mouse 🖱️✨
Move your hand to steer the cursor, tap to click. The IMU blends accelerometer + gyroscope for smooth rotation, with a touch of smoothing and a tiny dead-zone to kill jitter.
Gestures: tap = left click, double tap = double click, long press = right click.
Perfect for slides, couch browsing, or spinning 3D models. Still tuning, already fun.
