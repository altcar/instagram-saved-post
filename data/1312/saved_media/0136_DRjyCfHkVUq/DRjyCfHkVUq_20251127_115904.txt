💥 Introducing a new student-led design challenge for 2026! 

Today, we’re launching the AMplify Impact Challenge - a collaboration between @ewb_uk and @additive_manufacturing_uk.

Running from late January - early April, this team-based competition invites university students across the UK to explore how additive manufacturing and 3D printing can shape a better tomorrow - through bold design, sustainable thinking, and real-world prototyping.

In April, the top teams will pitch their concepts at the Grand Final at MACH 2026, NEC Birmingham, with the chance to work alongside industry mentors to develop working prototypes. 🏆

Head to link in bio for all the information, and to register your place.

ℹ️ Open to all year groups. No prior additive manufacturing experience required!
