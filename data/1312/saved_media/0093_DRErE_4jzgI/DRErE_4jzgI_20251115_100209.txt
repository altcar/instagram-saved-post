In the 1930s, workers digging in London uncovered something bizarre—dozens of ancient clay tablets buried under the city. 🏛️ What shocked them was that the tablets were written in cuneiform, the script of Mesopotamia, thousands of miles away. No one has ever explained how they ended up there.Some historians think they were brought over by collectors, others whisper about secret trade routes or even lost migrations. The tablets are now in the British Museum, sitting quietly as if mocking us with a reminder: history hides more than we’ll ever uncover. 📜👀
