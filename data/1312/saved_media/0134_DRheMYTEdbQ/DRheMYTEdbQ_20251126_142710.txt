A deadly blaze tore through several high-rise apartment towers in Hong Kong on Wednesday, killing at least 13 people and injuring 15 others, sending smoke billowing across the city’s northern New Territories and setting off an urgent rescue effort for an unknown number of people trapped inside.

The cause of the fire remained unknown. Hundreds of firefighters struggled to contain the inferno, which jumped across multiple high-rise towers. Several buildings were still ablaze late into the evening, more than six hours after the fire started.
