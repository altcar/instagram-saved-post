They fear being alone.
So they keep backups, side pieces, and “it’s not what you think” stories
ready at any moment.

No loyalty.
No accountability.
Just vibes, lies, and situationships that end the second truth shows up.

This generation…
yeah, it’s cooked. 🍳💀
