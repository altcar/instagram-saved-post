🎥 Plot:
Based on the real-life memoir of Jordan Belfort, the movie follows his rise from a young Wall Street stockbroker to a corrupt, drug-fueled, unbelievably rich mastermind of financial fraud.
As Jordan (Leonardo DiCaprio) and his insane partner Donnie Azoff (Jonah Hill) build a shady empire, things get out of control — fast. 🚁💰💊

Expect:

Wild parties

Outrageous scams

FBI chases

Legendary comedy moments (yes, the quaalude scene 😵‍💫)

All directed by Martin Scorsese with his trademark style.
