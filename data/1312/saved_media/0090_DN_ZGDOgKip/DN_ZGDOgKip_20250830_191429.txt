Stable Ronaldo or simply Ron, is an American Twitch streamer, YouTuber, and professional eSports player born on January 15, 2003, in the United States.  He gained prominence for his engaging Fortnite content and is recognized for his charismatic personality and interactive streams. 

Ronaldo began his streaming career in March 2019, initially gaining attention in August of that year through successful performances in Fortnite cash cups. His collaborations with players like MackWood and UnknownArmyx further elevated his status in the gaming community.  In August 2020, he signed with NRG eSports, a notable eSports organization, and remained with them until his departure in January 2022. 

On Twitch, Ronaldo has amassed over 3.7 million followers, with his channel ranking among the top for hours watched and average viewership.  His content primarily revolves around Fortnite, but he also engages in “Just Chatting” sessions, fostering a strong connection with his audience. Beyond Twitch, Ronaldo maintains a significant presence on YouTube, where he shares stream highlights and other content, further expanding his reach within the gaming community.
