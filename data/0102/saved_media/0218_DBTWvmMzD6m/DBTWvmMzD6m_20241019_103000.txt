If your company advertises various products or services, your brands will most likely fall into one of these brand architectures.

Which approach to take depends on your market segmentation and product portfolio.

Here is a simple summary for each.

1. Branded House: Single master brand spans all products and services, creating a unified identity.
2. Subbrands: Main brand supports distinct products with unique identities under its umbrella.
3. Endorsed Brands: Independent brands are endorsed by a master brand for credibility and recognition.
4. House of Brands: Multiple standalone brands operate independently without connection to a parent company.
5. Hybrid Brands: Combines elements of branded house and house of brands for flexibility across products or markets.
