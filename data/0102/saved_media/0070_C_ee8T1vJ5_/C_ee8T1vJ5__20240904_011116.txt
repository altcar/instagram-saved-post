Gordon’s recipe of the day 👇

A classic British dish with a savory meat filling topped with creamy mashed potatoes.

Cottage Pie

**Ingredients:**
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery sticks, diced
- 500 g (1 lb) ground beef or lamb
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup beef stock
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste

**For the mashed potatoes:**
- 1 kg (2 lbs) potatoes, peeled and chopped
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup milk
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender, about 15 minutes. Drain, mash with butter and milk, and season with salt and pepper.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Sauté the onion, carrots, and celery until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Add the ground beef or lamb, cooking until browned. Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, beef stock, thyme, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes until the mixture thickens slightly.
5. Stir in the frozen peas and transfer the meat mixture to a baking dish.
6. Spread the mashed potatoes evenly over the meat mixture, using a fork to create a textured surface.
7. Bake in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the top is golden and crisp.
8. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
