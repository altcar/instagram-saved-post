Look out for these codes at TKMaxx 👀👇🏻

When shopping there, always check the labels for a number ‘1’, ‘2’ or ‘7’.

A number 1 means that product was made specifically for TKMaxx, and may not be the same quality as the brand it says it is.

A number 2 is what you’re looking for! These items are made by the brand, and were sold by the brand (but are just cheaper being sold at TKMaxx). This is what you’re looking for.

There’s also a number 7, which means it’s last year’s stock (but still a branded item and genuine).

Please do have a check before you buy something. It may seem like a bargain looking at the price, but it may wear out sooner than you think if it’s not the real deal.
