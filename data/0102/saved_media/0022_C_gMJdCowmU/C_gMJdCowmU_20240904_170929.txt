Here are some brief characteristics of the GAZ-66:

- **Type:** Military truck
- **Manufacturer:** GAZ (Gorky Automobile Plant)
- **Production Years:** 1964–1999
- **Engine:** 4.25-liter V8 gasoline engine
- **Power:** Approximately 140 hp
- **Transmission:** 4-speed manual
- **Drive:** 6x6 all-wheel drive
- **Top Speed:** Around 80 km/h (50 mph)
- **Payload Capacity:** Up to 3,000 kg (6,600 lbs)
- **Fuel Capacity:** 120 liters (31.7 gallons)
- **Dimensions:** Length – 6.7 meters (22 feet), Width – 2.5 meters (8.2 feet), Height – 2.9 meters (9.5 feet)
- **Usage:** Military, utility, and off-road applications

The GAZ-66 is known for its durability and off-road capability.
