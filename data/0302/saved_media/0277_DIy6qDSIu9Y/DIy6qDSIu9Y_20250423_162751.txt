Did you know what these codes mean? 👀👇🏻

1) If you see codes such as “D27” on fruit & veg - that’s the expiry date of that item.

For the letter, A = January, B = February and so on.

So in this case “D27” means an expiry date of April 27th. If you need items to last longer, grab a longer date to ensure it stays fresh!

2) If you see a big lower case ‘e’ next to a weight, it means it’s estimated, so could be more or less than it says.

If you’re super tight, you can use the scales for bags of fruit to find the biggest weight 😆

3) Clubcard offers at Tesco have expiry dates in code on their shelves.

Find a 6 digit number that starts “25”. The last two grouped digits are the day, the middle two are the month, and the first two are the year.

So if it says 250422… it expires 22/04/25.
