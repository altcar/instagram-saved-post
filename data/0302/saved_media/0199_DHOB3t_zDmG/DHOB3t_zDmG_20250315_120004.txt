Parm Dhoot & Tye Davis: Innovators Behind MuteMe’s Productivity Revolution.

Parm Dhoot and Tye Davis co-founded MuteMe, a game-changing device designed to make remote work and virtual communication more seamless. Their illuminated mute button helps professionals, students, and streamers avoid awkward moments by providing a clear visual cue when their microphone is on or off. After gaining traction through crowdfunding and a successful pitch on Shark Tank, MuteMe has become a must-have tool in the hybrid work era.

Like many entrepreneurs, their journey wasn’t without challenges—securing funding, navigating supply chain issues, and educating the market on their product’s necessity were hurdles they had to overcome. But through persistence and innovation, they’ve built a brand that enhances productivity worldwide.
