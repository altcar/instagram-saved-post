In various major supermarkets in the UK, meatball products commonly contain a preservative called sulphite. 

Sulphite poses potential risks for sensitive individuals, including children, such as dermatitis, urticaria, asthmatic reactions, abdominal pain and diarrhoea. The severity of these reactions varies from person to person, with higher risks for patients and children with chronic asthma.

It is advisable to avoid consumption as much as possible. Opt for fresh meat whenever feasible. Packaged meat products can be selected as pre-cut fresh meat or minced meat as they generally contain fewer preservatives. 

Supermarket frozen seafood, dried fruits, and other products may also contain sulphite. Before purchasing, it is recommended to read the nutrition labels carefully and check for the presence of the following terms: Sulphite, Sulphur Dioxide, Potassium/Sodium Bisulphite.
