Emma Watson has been banned from driving for six months, after she was caught speeding in Oxford.
 
The court heard that Emma drove 38mph in a 30mph zone in July last year and already had nine points on her licence before the speeding incident occurred.
 
The actress is currently studying for a master's degree in creative writing at the University of Oxford, and has been since 2023.
