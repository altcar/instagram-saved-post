Make Your Shots Come Alive!

Want your shots to feel more cinematic and engaging?
Try these 10 powerful camera movements with just one actor to add depth, emotion, and motion to your scenes:

1. Follow Shot – Camera follows the actor from behind or the side. Creates a sense of journey.

2. Lead Shot – Camera moves backward as the actor walks forward. Builds intimacy and tension.

3. Walk & Talk – Classic dialogue shot with the actor walking and talking. Natural and fluid.

4. Over-the-Shoulder Tracking – Tracking from behind the shoulder. Immersive and personal.

5. 360 Around the Actor – Camera circles the actor. Great for drama or inner conflict.

6. Push-In (Dolly In) – Camera slowly moves in. Builds emotion and focus.

7. Pull-Out (Dolly Out) – Camera moves away. Suggests isolation or realization.

8. Side Tracking – Camera moves sideways alongside the actor. Perfect for motion and space.

9. Crane with Subject – Vertical movement with the actor. Adds scale and visual drama.

10. Handheld with Actor – Unstable, raw movement. Feels real, urgent, or emotional.

🎥 These techniques work even in small spaces or low-budget setups!
