Minecraft police station? Why it’s more than just blocks 👇

It’s not always easy to know what’s happening in our kids’ online worlds. Watching this video together can be a helpful way to start talking about gaming and the kinds of risks they might face.

Here are a few tips and conversation starters:
🕹️ Ask about their gaming, what games they enjoy, who they’re playing with, and what they like about it. This could also help you understand their gaming world better.
🔐 Discuss online safety and why it’s important to keep personal information private. Remind them that not everyone online is who they say they are, and why they shouldn’t share their real name, address, or school with online friends.
🧍 Remind them they can come to you if they see or experience bullying or anything that doesn’t feel right. They could chat to a friend, parent, or trusted adult.

For advice and support about online safety, visit Childline.
