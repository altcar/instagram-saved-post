HOTEL SOAP➡️SAVING LIVES 🙌 Share this video to support this amazing project! 🧼

5 million bars of soap are thrown away every day by hotels in the United States alone! This company is turning this waste into new soap and is donating it to those who need it.

We recently visited one of @clean_the_world’s recycling operations centres in Florida and were amazed by the incredible work they are doing. If you like seeing projects like this make sure to follow along @goinggreenmedia 💚

The soap is sorted, refined and sanitised before being compressed into new bars, and with the help of Carlos, who’s been here almost since the beginning, they have so far managed to upcycle 85 million bars of soap!

They are also constantly partnering with other small businesses innovating different ways to recycle the plastic bottles from hotel toiletries to create things like new, refillable bottles and even bricks for construction!

The soap bars are sent to various international NGO partners providing access to hygiene products to millions, as well as are used in bigger hygiene kits sent around the world.

In response to the recent LA fires, Clean the World’s partner NGO, @the_wash_foundation have sent thousands of hygiene kits to those impacted in California. 

Hygiene-related illnesses are among the top two causes of childhood mortality worldwide.

Solutions like this are having a huge impact on not just reducing waste, but also in saving lives!
