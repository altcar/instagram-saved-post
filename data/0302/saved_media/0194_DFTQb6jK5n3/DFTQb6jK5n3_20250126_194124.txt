Chinese SAUCES explained in the caption
💚 In today’s video we are introducing some of the Chinese sauces that you come across at the Asian store and how you can use it. Comment in below and let us know which one is your favorite and how you like to use it😋

1.Vegan oyster sauce
Simply a must-have. Vegan oyster sauce is made with shiitake mushrooms, it adds a good savoury taste and extra umami to the ingredients 

2.Hong Kong style sweet soy sauce
This is a seasoned soy sauce that is sweeter and richer in taste than a regular soy sauce. It’s the perfect seasoning to pour over many Cantonese dim-sums while serving, rice rolls such as “cheung fun” and clay pot rice

3.Soybean Sauce
This soybean sauce is savoury and rich, it adds great flavours to the ingredients , it’s good in stir-fries, dips, and noodle or rice dishes

4. Pixian Broad Bean Paste
This fermented broad bean paste is the soul of Sichuan cooking, it is very savoury, tangy and slightly spicy, it brings the unique Sichuan taste to dishes

5.Black Bean Sauce
This Cantonese sauce is made with fermented black beans, it adds the unique savoury and fermented taste to the ingredients, it’s great to for steamed dishes or stir-fries

6&7 Hoisin Sauce&Gluten Free Hoisin Sauce
Hoisin sauce is fundamental in Cantonese cooking, it has a sweet, savoury and smoky taste, it adds umami and good flavours to the ingredients 

8. Vegan Sacha Sauce
The original sacha sauce contains shrimps, this one is made with soy protein and spices. This sauce is very rich in flavours and aromatic, it’s great in noodles, rice, stir-fries and barbecue
