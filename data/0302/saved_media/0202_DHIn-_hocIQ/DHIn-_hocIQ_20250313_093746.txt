M&S has just shaken up the cereal aisle with its boldest breakfast move yet: one ingredient cornflakes.

🌽 No sugar, no salt, no preservatives, no additives. Just 325g of corn.

🌽 And they’ve stuck the recipe right on the front of the box – a move which will certainly raise the game for ingredient transparency from its competitors and other food brands.
