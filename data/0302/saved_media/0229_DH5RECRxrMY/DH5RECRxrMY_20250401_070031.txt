🧡 SHEFFIELD COULD GAIN A NEW SAUCY STATUE 🧡⁠
⁠
The greatest sauce in all the land is finally getting the recognition it deserves, right in its birth city Sheffield.⁠
⁠
Planning permission has been submitted for a 9ft statue of @hendersonsrelish sauce to be situated slap bang in the centre over at the Peace Gardens.⁠
⁠
Not only can it be enjoyed on your pies, chips, stews and gravy but also with a splash of jet water from The Goodwin Fountain - yummy. 🤤⁠
⁠
In the words of arcticmonkey's Matt Helders, "It's like Worcester sauce, but one million times better." Exactly matt, and won't it look stellar for us all to see?⁠
