Hong Kong’s tramway and the advertising is unique in two ways. 

First, the design of the trams is influenced by Britain, due to its colonial history. Second, the positive attitude toward advertising is influenced by the economic system and the strong presence of the finance sector in Hong Kong.

Both factors shape how brands advertise on trams. For example, full top-to-bottom ad wraps are less common in London. Double-decker buses there usually feature horizontal banners instead. I’m not saying 360° wraps don’t exist in the west, but they’re not the go-to ad format and are usually reserved for movie promotions.

We talked about bus wraps in part one or two of this series, Advertising Is Everywhere.

Overall, I really like how advertising blends into Hong Kong trams, and fashion brands have done some of their best work in the city. The City has also seen some good campaigns from publishers like Bloomberg, FT, and The Economist.
