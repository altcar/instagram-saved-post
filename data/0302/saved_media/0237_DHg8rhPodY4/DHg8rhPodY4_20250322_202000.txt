When selecting bread in supermarkets, it is important to be mindful of certain ingredients to avoid. Some common brands contain a preservative called Calcium Propionate, which poses risks when consumed frequently.
 
1. Behavioural Effects on Children Research indicates that daily intake of Calcium Propionate in bread or other foods by children may lead to irritability, restlessness, inattention and sleep disturbance. 
2. Neurological Issues A study on rats suggested that the neurotoxicity of Propionate could play a central role in the etiology of autistic biochemical features.
 
3. Metabolic Impact
The metabolic impact of Propionate potentially increases the risk for diabetes and obesity in humans.
