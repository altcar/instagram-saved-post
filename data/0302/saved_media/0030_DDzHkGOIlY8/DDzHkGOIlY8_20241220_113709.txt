IS THE BIGGEST TOURIST TRAP IN PARIS OVERRATED!? 🇫🇷 

We left disappointed with our visit to the famous entrecôte in Paris. Whilst we have had some great meals here in other cities, we found the original in Paris to be very slap dash. Steak was very chewy (usually the slices of steak are a lot thinner and well rested), the sauce obviously is great and the chips were great with some bites but other bites soft with little seasoning. 

Whilst this certainly won’t be our list visit to an Entrocote (especially in London), I wouldn’t rush back here and queue for so long!
