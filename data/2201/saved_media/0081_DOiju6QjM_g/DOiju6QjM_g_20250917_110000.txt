NOW THAT’S A CHEAP BITE TO EAT 🥯

Finding tasty food that doesn’t break the bank can be tricky, but we’ve rounded up our favourite spots in Sheffield that serve up delicious grub without leaving your wallet burning.

For lunchtime sandwiches and bagels, the city has you covered, as @bestboybagels , and @twisteddeli are all dishing out options far better than your average meal deal. 🥪

And of course, we can’t forget @cambridge_street_collective for its affordable eats. Imagine grabbing a pasta from @renatapasta_official for just £10, or an iconic cheesy corn dog from @clapping_seoul 

Sweet tooth? Don’t worry - @paradisotiramisu serves up a huge slice of tiramisu for under £10. Now that’s a bargain worth celebrating.

It’s pretty clear where my money’s going this weekend 💸
