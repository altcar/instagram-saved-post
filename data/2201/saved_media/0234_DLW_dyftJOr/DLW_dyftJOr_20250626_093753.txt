A huge congratulations to BA Business Management and Economics with Placement Year student Maria Jose Gordillo on being awarded The University of Sheffield's Chancellor's Medal 2025!

The Medal is awarded each year to a final year student or recent graduate in recognition of outstanding achievements in academia, sporting activities or other personal achievements. Maria is particularly recognised for her outstanding leadership and exceptional commitment to global sustainability and inclusivity.
