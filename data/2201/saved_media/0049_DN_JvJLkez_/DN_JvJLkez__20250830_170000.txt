Paris Jackson, born April 3, 1998, is the only daughter of Michael Jackson and Debbie Rowe. Raised by her father after her parents’ 1999 divorce, she grew up in the spotlight, shielded from much of the public eye. Following Michael’s death in 2009, Paris lived with her grandmother, Katherine Jackson, and pursued modeling, acting, and music, carving her own path in entertainment.

At 15, Paris faced mental health struggles, including a publicized suicide attempt in 2013, which she later addressed openly, advocating for mental health awareness. She formed The Soundflowers, a folk-pop band, and released music in 2020. Paris continues to honor her father’s legacy while establishing her own identity.
