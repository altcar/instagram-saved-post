The cross that crowns her aching brow
Is sharpened now—a vow, a vow,
To guard the faith through pain and chain,
Yet bleed in silence, not complain.

Her ribs of gold, her teeth of night,
Catch every fragment of the light,
But nothing shines beneath her skin—
It’s hollow where the soul had been.

Once flesh and mercy graced her face,
Now death has claimed the sacred place,
And though her eyes look heaven’s way,
She knows the Lord has turned away.

Kneel not for her, this mourning bride,
For heaven’s doors have closed inside;
She prays, but all her words decay,
In halls where God will never stay.
