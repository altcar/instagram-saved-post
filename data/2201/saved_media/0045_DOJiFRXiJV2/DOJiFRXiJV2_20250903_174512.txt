Hamid Naderi Yeganeh is an Iranian mathematical artist and digital creator based in Iran and currently at UCL.

He produces mesmerizing images—birds, fish, butterflies, faces, fractals, and more—using nothing but mathematical formulas like trigonometric, exponential, Fibonacci-based, and sawtooth functions. His process involves generating thousands of random curves until a hidden form emerges, which he then fine-tunes, or carefully constructing shapes step by step through structured equations.

His works such as “A Bird in Flight” and “9000 Ellipses” have graced the cover of The American Mathematical Monthly and other prestigious outlets, and his creations are also found in Oxford University Press math textbooks.

Each image is fully describable by equations—he uses math itself as both his paintbrush and his canvas, blending precision with surprising creativity.
