🎬 The Favourite (2018)
🎥 Genre: Comedy, Thriller, Historical Drama
⏳ Runtime: 2h 0m
🎬 Directed by: Yorgos Lanthimos
📝 Written by: Deborah Davis, Tony McNamara
⭐ Starring: Olivia Colman, Emma Stone, Rachel Weisz, Nicholas Hoult, Joe Alwyn

Overview:

Set in early 18th-century England, The Favourite follows the power struggle between two cousins, Sarah Churchill (Rachel Weisz) and Abigail Masham (Emma Stone), as they vie for the favor of the frail and unpredictable Queen Anne (Olivia Colman). Filled with sharp wit, political intrigue, and dark humor, the film explores themes of manipulation, ambition, and desire in a visually stunning period setting. Olivia Colman’s performance as Queen Anne earned her an Academy Award for Best Actress.
