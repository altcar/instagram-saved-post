Three Russian students have been expelled from the Moscow State Academy of Arts and Industry after a video of them twerking in front of the Cathedral of Christ the Saviour went viral on social media, prompting Orthodox activists to demand their expulsion, Russian human rights group OVD-Info reported on Monday.

Three first-year students posted the video of their dance on TikTok at the beginning of summer. Soon after, Russian orthodox activist group Sorok Sorokov urged its followers to denounce the young women for “insulting the feelings of believers” and write letters to the Investigative Committee, asking for a criminal case to be opened against them.

Three days later, it was announced that the girls had been expelled. One of the academy’s professors told his students at the time to watch their behaviour and make sure their lives weren't “ruined because of a stupid mistake”. In the end, the young women faced administrative charges for petty hooliganism.
