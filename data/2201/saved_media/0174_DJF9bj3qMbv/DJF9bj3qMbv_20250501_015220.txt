Pranking strangers is one of those bold moves that walks the line between “uh-oh” and comedy gold. At first, they’re confused or even mildly annoyed—brows furrowed, arms crossed, ready to report you to someone… anyone. But the moment they realize it’s all in good fun, you can actually see their whole vibe shift from “Who is this clown?” to full-blown laughter. It’s the universal reminder that humor breaks barriers, and sometimes, all it takes is a harmless trick to turn a random stranger into your biggest fan—for like, five minutes. And hey, that’s a pretty solid five minutes.
