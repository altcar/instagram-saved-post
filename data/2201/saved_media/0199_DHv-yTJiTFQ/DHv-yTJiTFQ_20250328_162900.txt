Ever wonder why some medicines need to be mixed or crushed before taking? It’s not bad design—it’s brilliant marketing. 

Pharma companies realized that adding friction made people remember to take their pills, turning a simple task into a ritual. 

Sometimes, adding a little friction turns a product into a belief and makes a user a loyalist.
