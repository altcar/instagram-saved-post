Here’s to the immigrants ✈️

No one prepares you for how hard it is to start life from 0. Moving to a new country and leaving your family behind. Having no job, no money, and no network. Trying to navigate how to get through all the paperwork. The endless obstacles and trials for trying to make life worth how hard you’re working. 

But what no one can tell you is what it teaches you. It teaches you to always find a way. To not look for opportunities but to create them. You fight harder than everyone else for a good job because being able to earn money is a blessing when most people won’t hire you. And every day is a battle between how much you miss home but how much you want to stay here and make the most of what you have. 

Being an immigrant from Australia my experience looks a lot different from most, but I can’t emphasise the struggles I faced on my own journey enough. Getting into college was a dream that seemed unreachable. Getting my first job that payed me $10 an hour was one of the best feelings in the world. Finally having enough money for a car after having to get everywhere by my bike and sometime wasting money on uber. All these struggles were blessings in disguise and I wouldn’t change them for the world ❤️
